# Resources

## Dictionaries and Reference

- **Riggs, S.R. — Dakota-English Dictionary** (1890, Smithsonian) — The foundational Dakota dictionary; available free online at archive.org. Outdated orthography but comprehensive.
- **Williamson, T.S. — Dakota-English Dictionary** — An earlier missionary dictionary; useful for comparison.
- **New Lakota Dictionary** — Covers Lakota (closely related); useful for cognates and cross-reference.

## Language Learning

- **Dakota Language Institute** — The leading academic center for Dakota language research and curriculum.
- **Dakhóta Iápi Okhódakičhiye (Dakota Language Society)** — Community organization promoting Dakota language revitalization.
- **Wóiyake Magazine** — Dakota-language materials and teaching resources.
- **Bdote Learning Center, Minneapolis** — Urban Dakota language and culture education.

## History and Culture (AIS 112 Context)

- **Westerman, Gwen & Bruce White. *Mni Sota Makoce: The Land of the Dakota*** — Essential, accessible history of Dakota people in Minnesota.
- **Wingerd, Mary Lethert. *North Country: The Making of Minnesota*** — Chapter on Dakota history and the 1862 war.
- **Chomsky, Carol. "The United States–Dakota War Trials"** — Legal analysis of the 1862 military tribunals (Stanford Law Review, 1990).
- **Anderson, Gary Clayton. *Kinsmen of Another Kind: Dakota-White Relations in the Upper Mississippi Valley*** — Pre-contact through treaty era.

## Primary Sources on the 1862 War

- **Minnesota Historical Society — U.S.-Dakota War of 1862** — mnopedia.org/article/u-s-dakota-war-1862
- **Dakota 38 Documentary** (2012, Smooth Feather Productions) — Feature documentary on the Wokiksuye memorial ride; widely available.
- **National Native American Boarding School Healing Coalition** — boardingschoolhealing.org

## Treaty Rights and Legal Context

- **1851 Treaty of Traverse des Sioux** — Full text available at the Minnesota Historical Society
- **1851 Treaty of Mendota** — The second 1851 treaty
- **1858 Minnesota Treaties** — Established current reservation boundaries
- **Mille Lacs Band v. Minnesota (1999)** — Supreme Court case affirming 1837 treaty hunting and fishing rights

## Land and Maps

- **Native Land Digital** (native-land.ca) — Interactive map showing Dakota territories, treaty areas, and language regions
- **Bdóte Memory Map** (bdotememorymap.org) — Interactive map of the Dakota homeland in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area with historical layers

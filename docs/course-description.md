# Course Description

## Dakhotiyapi — Beginning Dakota Language (Year 1)

**Level:** High School Introductory  
**Duration:** 160 instructional days (one academic year)  
**Prerequisites:** None  
**Cross-listed with:** AIS 112 — American Indian Studies

---

## Course Overview

This course introduces students to the Dakota language alongside the history, culture, and sovereignty of the Očhéthi Šakówiŋ (Seven Council Fires). Each chapter pairs language learning with AIS 112 content, reinforcing that Dakota language and Dakota history are inseparable.

## Semester Structure

### Semester 1 (Chapters 1–4)

| Chapter | Dakota Title | Topics | AIS 112 Context |
|---------|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Háu / Haŋ | Greetings, identity, tiyóšpaye | Očhéthi Šakówiŋ, pre-contact society |
| 2 | Otóiyohi | Numbers, colors, descriptors | Trade networks, wampum, counting systems |
| 3 | Wóuŋsipi | Daily life, action verbs | Seasonal calendar, bison economy |
| 4 | Čhaŋté | Body, health, relationships | Medicine traditions, kinship systems |

**Mid-Year Exam** — Chapters 1–4 | ACTFL Novice Low–Mid

### Semester 2 (Chapters 5–8)

| Chapter | Dakota Title | Topics | AIS 112 Context |
|---------|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| 5 | Wóčhekiye | Prayer, values, the sacred | Mitákuye Oyásʼiŋ, spiritual traditions |
| 6 | Makóčhe | Land, seasons, directions | Seasonal lifeways, celestial knowledge |
| 7 | Wóoyake | Story, ceremony, oral tradition | Oral tradition, ceremony, suppression |
| 8 | Oyáte | Nation, treaties, sovereignty | 1862 War, Fort Snelling, treaty rights |

**End-of-Year Exam** — Chapters 5–8 | ACTFL Novice Mid–High

## ACTFL Proficiency Targets

| Level | Description | Target |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Novice Low | Isolated words and memorized phrases | Chapters 1–2 |
| Novice Mid | Lists of words, some phrase combining | Chapters 3–4 / Mid-Year |
| Novice High | Short phrases, some spontaneous production | Chapters 5–7 |
| Intermediate Low | Simple sentences, creates with language | Chapter 8 / End of Year |

## The Očhéthi Šakówiŋ — Seven Council Fires

The Dakota course situates the language within the broader Očhéthi Šakówiŋ (Seven Council Fires), the political and cultural alliance of seven related peoples:

| Council Fire | Common Name | Dialect |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Bdewákhaŋthuŋwaŋ | Mdewakanton | Dakota |
| Waȟpékhute | Wahpekute | Dakota |
| Sisíthuŋwaŋ | Sisseton | Dakota |
| Waȟpéthuŋwaŋ | Wahpeton | Dakota |
| Iháŋkthuŋwaŋ | Yankton | Nakota |
| Iháŋkthuŋwaŋna | Yanktonai | Nakota |
| Thítȟuŋwaŋ | Teton / Lakota | Lakota |

This course teaches **Dakota** (the eastern dialect), the language of the Minnesota communities.

# Exams Overview

Two major exams — mid-year and end-of-year — each including an ACTFL proficiency self-assessment.

## Exam Schedule

| Exam | Covers | Format | ACTFL Target |
|------|--------|--------|--------------|
| [Mid-Year Exam](midyear.md) | Chapters 1–4 | 15 MC + 10 fill + 5 translation + essay | Novice Low → Novice Mid |
| [End-of-Year Exam](endofyear.md) | Chapters 5–8 (+ cumulative) | 15 MC + 10 fill + 5 translation + essay | Novice Mid → Novice High |

## Exam Preparation Tips

1. Review chapter vocabulary tables and the [Glossary](../glossary.md)
2. Practice SOV word order — put the verb last in every sentence you write
3. Know the future *-kte* and negation *-šni* suffixes and how to apply them
4. Review AIS 112 historical content — both exams include cultural questions
5. Use chapter quizzes as practice runs — same question formats
6. Study the gender speech distinction (*Háu* / *Haŋ* and sentence-final particles)

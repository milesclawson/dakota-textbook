## Part A — Multiple Choice (30 points)

??? question "1. Which greeting does a Dakota man use?"
    **A) Háu** | B) Haŋ | C) Pidámayaye | D) Háŋ | **Answer: A**

??? question "2. The Dakota word order is:"
    A) SVO (Subject-Verb-Object) like English  
    **B) SOV (Subject-Object-Verb) — verb comes last**  
    C) VSO (Verb-Subject-Object)  
    D) Free order with no pattern  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "3. How do you form the future tense in Dakota?"
    A) Add a prefix *wí-* before the verb  
    **B) Add the suffix *-kte* after the verb**  
    C) Change the vowel of the verb root  
    D) Use a separate auxiliary word  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "4. The color 'red' as a stative verb in Dakota is:"
    **A) šá**  
    B) tó  
    C) sápa  
    D) ğí  
    **Answer: A — šá**

??? question "5. 'My name is ___' in Dakota uses the construction:"
    A) ___ emítiyapi  
    **B) ___ emáčiyapi**  
    C) ___ etáŋhaŋ waúŋ  
    D) ___ mitiyóšpaye  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "6. Numbers in Dakota greater than ten are formed by:"
    A) Using separate words for each digit  
    **B) Using *wikčémna* (ten) with a multiplier, then adding units**  
    C) Borrowing English number words  
    D) Using a completely separate number system for teens  
    **Answer: B**  
    e.g., *wikčémna núŋpa* (twenty) + *ákeyaŋ waŋží* (plus one) = 21

??? question "7. 'I do not eat' in Dakota uses:"
    A) Hiyá + verb  
    **B) The negation suffix *-šni* on the verb**  
    C) A separate word *né* before the verb  
    D) Reversing the SOV order  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "8. The tiyóšpaye is:"
    A) A type of food storage  
    **B) The extended family band — the basic social unit**  
    C) A ceremonial lodge  
    D) The Dakota national council  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "9. Dakota stative verbs for colors:"
    A) Must always follow the noun they describe  
    **B) Function as predicates — 'the dog is-black' not 'the black dog'**  
    C) Are identical in all grammatical persons  
    D) Only apply to animate nouns  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "10. 'My heart' in Dakota:"
    A) čhaŋté mítawa  
    **B) mičháŋte**  
    C) čhaŋté emáčiyapi  
    D) mitiyóšpaye čhaŋté  
    **Answer: B — mičháŋte** (possessive prefix *mi-* + *čhaŋté*)

??? question "11. How do you say 'I am sick / something hurts me' in Dakota?"
    A) Wičhášta mayázaŋ  
    **B) Mayázaŋ** (lit. "it pains me")  
    C) Sápa mayázaŋ  
    D) Mičháŋte yá  
    **Answer: B — Mayázaŋ**

??? question "12. The Očhéthi Šakówiŋ consists of how many council fires?"
    A) 4 | B) 5 | **C) 7** | D) 13 | **Answer: C**

??? question "13. 'You're welcome / it's nothing' in Dakota:"
    A) Pidámayaye  
    B) Tokhéya  
    **C) Tókheča šni**  
    D) Háŋ  
    **Answer: C — Tókheča šni** (lit. "it's not different")

??? question "14. Pre-contact Dakota leadership was based on:"
    A) Hereditary chieftainship  
    **B) Earned respect through wisdom, bravery, and generosity**  
    C) Military conquest  
    D) Religious appointment  
    **Answer: B**

??? question "15. Which word means 'woman' in Dakota?"
    A) Wíčhaša  
    **B) Wiŋyaŋ**  
    C) Oyáte  
    D) Akíčhita  
    **Answer: B — Wiŋyaŋ**

## Part B — Fill in the Blank (30 points)

16. The Dakota number for "seven": **_______________**
17. Dakota word order: Subject — **_______________** — Verb
18. To negate a Dakota verb, add the suffix **_______________**
19. The stative verb for "it is black" in Dakota: **_______________**
20. "Thank you" in Dakota: **_______________**
21. The extended family band in Dakota society: **_______________**
22. Future tense suffix in Dakota: **_______________**
23. "I am from ___" in Dakota: **_______________ etáŋhaŋ waúŋ**
24. "It hurts / I am sick" in Dakota: **_______________**
25. The four seasons: Spring **_____________**, Summer **_____________**, Fall **_____________**, Winter **_____________**

??? answer "Part B Answers"
    16. šakówiŋ | 17. Object | 18. -šni | 19. sápa | 20. Pidámayaye  
    21. Tiyóšpaye | 22. -kte | 23. [place name] | 24. Mayázaŋ  
    25. wétȟo | blokétu | ptaŋyétu | waníyetu

## Part C — Translation (20 points)

Translate each sentence into English (4 pts each):

26. *James emáčiyapi. Bdewákhaŋthuŋwaŋ etáŋhaŋ waúŋ.*
27. *Šúŋka waŋ sápa yá.*
28. *Waŋží, núŋpa, yámni, tópa, záptaŋ.*
29. *Mičháŋte mayázaŋ. Pidámayaye.*
30. *Wičhášta kiŋ wóuŋsipi-kte.*

??? answer "Part C Answers"
    26. My name is James. I am from Mdewakanton.  
    27. A dog is black. (There is a black dog.)  
    28. One, two, three, four, five.  
    29. My heart hurts. Thank you.  
    30. The man will work / do daily activities.

## Part D — Essay (20 points)

**Choose ONE prompt. Write 8–12 sentences. Underline at least 5 Dakota words used correctly.**

**Option A:** Explain the tiyóšpaye system and pre-contact Dakota political structure. How does the language reflect Dakota social values (passive name construction, gender speech, etc.)?

**Option B:** Explain the SOV word order and the stative verb system for colors in Dakota. Give 4 example sentences showing both features, with translations.

**Option C:** Describe what you would say in a formal Dakota introduction — name, origin, and closing. Explain the cultural significance of each element and how it differs from an English introduction.
